Related Work (4): Fukiage's Visibility Predictor

The model assumes a single 2D display — not OST-AR.

Four OST-AR-specific gaps:

  • ① Accommodation  —  induces defocus blur.
  • ② Binocular disparity  —  arises with depth; fusion is also a challenge.
  • ③ BG discounting  —  observers partly ignore the real BG.
  • ④ Optical addition  —  linear summation yields HDR-range light.

→ This work bridges these gaps, extending the model into an OST-AR visibility predictor.

Fukiage & Oishi, ACM TAP 2023

Method: Overview

Three-step approach to extend Fukiage's model for OST-AR.
  1. STEP 1 Visibility Evaluation Experiment  —  measure thresholds on a real OST-AR setup.
  2. STEP 2 Model Extension  —  incorporate four OST-AR-specific factors.
  3. STEP 3 Evaluation of Extended Model  —  validate against measured data.
11 / 23

Method (1): Visibility Evaluation Experiment

Identify which OST-AR phenomena significantly affect visibility — to guide model extension.

Perceptual phenomena under study:

  • Defocus blur on FG and BG.
  • Uncrossed disparity & binocular fusion  —  alter BG perception & induce BG discounting.
  • Linear luminance summation  —  alters apparent contrast.

→ Decide which extensions to implement and fit their parameters.

12 / 23

Method (2): Model Extension

Extend Fukiage's pipeline with four OST-AR-specific mechanisms.
① Defocus blur
PSF h(u; ΔD, p) applied per layer.
② Disparity & fusion
Binocular gain control under uncrossed disparity.
③ Optical addition
Iretina = IFG + α·IBG (linear sum).
④ BG discounting
Down-weight BG with α ≈ 0.2.

→ Each gap from RW (4) is addressed by a dedicated module.

Preliminary Experiment: Objective

Measure how visibility of an FG Gabor changes with spatial frequency, luminance, depth, and ocularity.

Why a Gabor patch?

  • Localized in space and frequency — single CSF channel.
  • Standard stimulus for contrast detection & masking.
  • Tests the additive-mixture and defocus terms directly.

Discussion

  • Frequency dependence matches the CSF prediction.
  • Defocus acts as a low-pass on BG, as the PSF predicts.
  • Binocular condition shows clear summation.
  • Limit: only Gabor stimuli; natural images not yet tested.
Findings give parameter constraints for the input-stage extensions.

Future Work & Conclusion

Main experiment  (toward validation)

  • Natural FG overlays on natural BG photos.
  • 2-AFC threshold + supra-threshold rating.
  • Compare human ratings vs. model predictions.

Take-home

  • OST-AR visibility depends strongly on the BG.
  • We extend Fukiage's input stage with four OST-AR factors.
  • Gabor data already constrain the extension's parameters.
